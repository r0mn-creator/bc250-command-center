⚠ Proceed at your own risk

This is the BC-250 Command Center. It writes directly to low-level hardware registers and firmware-level SMU settings — CPU/GPU clock, voltage, and compute-unit routing. Incorrect values can crash the system, cause instability, or in the case of CPU voltage/frequency overclocking, permanently damage the hardware.

Be careful with changes. Know what a control does before you click it.

Not every BC-250 can unlock all 40 GPU compute units or all CPU cores — most can, but it depends on the individual chip and isn't guaranteed.

Every action here is logged and reversible via config files — except physical hardware damage from CPU overvoltage, which is not.
